# Hashtags, Titles, Descriptions and Metadata for Spanish-language YouTube Shorts (2025-2026)

Context: "Historia en Cubitos" posts Spanish comedy-explainer Shorts for an audience in Peru and LatAm. Each description currently has the title, a fixed channel line, a "Fuentes:" list of URLs and 3 hashtags. Research date: 2026-10-30. Labels used below: **[OFFICIAL]** means YouTube Help or a YouTube employee; **[CREATOR/VENDOR]** means a third-party blog or tool maker, which is opinion or heuristic.

## 1. Do hashtags matter for Shorts discovery? How many, where, is #Shorts useful, and what are the rules?

### Takeaway
Hashtags are a weak, secondary signal. YouTube's own Shorts lead said they are "not required" but may carry weight for trending topics. Retention and swipe behaviour drive Shorts distribution. Use 3 to 5 relevant hashtags at the end of the description. #Shorts is optional, because YouTube now detects Shorts by format. Stay far below the 60-hashtag cap, and every hashtag must relate to the video.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] Hashtags can go in the title or the description. "Up to three hashtags that are considered most engaging will appear by your video title," chosen from the description's hashtags. — [YouTube Help: hashtag policies](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en)
- [OFFICIAL] The cap: "If a video or playlist has more than 60 hashtags, we'll ignore each hashtag on that content." — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en)
- [OFFICIAL] Misleading hashtags: "Don't add hashtags that are not directly related to the video." Also prohibited: hashtags without spaces that are used as harassment, hate, sexual or vulgar terms; profanity can lead to age restriction or removal. Hashtags that violate policy "won't show below your content, and may be removed." — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en)
- [OFFICIAL] "Adding ordinary descriptive tags or repetitive sentences in the description is (still) prohibited." This sits on the same hashtag policy page. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en)
- [OFFICIAL] The Shorts editor suggests hashtags as you type, and hashtags you used before show a clock icon. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en)
- [OFFICIAL, 2023] Todd Sherman, who led Shorts product, said in a Creator Insider interview that hashtags are not required but could carry weight, especially for content on a current global trending topic. This is a paraphrase from a German summary. — [OnlineMarketing.de, Aug/Sep 2023](https://onlinemarketing.de/social-media-marketing/youtube-shorts-algorithmus-creator-insights)
- [Secondary] Metricool attributes a similar statement to Todd Beaupré: hashtags on Shorts "no son obligatorios y por tanto no determinan el éxito." The attribution is unverified and may be the Sherman interview. — [Metricool ES](https://metricool.com/es/algoritmo-youtube-shorts)
- [Historical] YouTube's older Shorts explainer told creators to include #Shorts "to help with discovery." In January 2023 Social Media Today noted this might be outdated, because Shorts are now auto-classified, and YouTube did not answer when asked. — [Social Media Today, 2023-01-05](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/youtube-experiments-with-suggested-hashtags-for-shorts-clips/639748/)
- [OFFICIAL] Shorts are now "short-form videos that are up to 3 minutes long," and titles have a 100-character maximum. Shorts are classified by format, not by hashtag. — [YouTube Help: Upload Shorts](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070?hl=en)
- [CREATOR/VENDOR] Subscribr recommends 3 to 6 targeted hashtags, placed at the bottom of the description rather than in the title. It calls #Shorts optional, saying many large creators skip it, and prefers niche hashtags to broad ones like #viral and #funny. — [Subscribr](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-shorts-hashtags-virality)
- [CREATOR/VENDOR, Feb 2026, Spanish] Schedulala recommends 3 to 5 hashtags at the end of the description, never in the middle. It structures them as one broad, one or two niche and one or two specific tags. It says performance drops above 8 and that 15 or more "activa filtros de spam," with no data given. It also says to avoid TikTok tags like #fyp and to use CamelCase. — [Schedulala ES](https://schedulala.com/es/blog/hashtags-youtube-shorts-mejores-practicas)
- [CREATOR/VENDOR] Captions suggests 3 to 5. Clipchamp says to put hashtags in the title and the description, and calls traditional tags low-value in 2025. — [Captions](https://www.captions.ai/blog-post/hashtags-for-youtube-shorts); [Clipchamp](https://clipchamp.com/en/blog/youtube-tags-ultimate-guide/)

### Inferences
- The channel's current count of 3 hashtags is fine. Going to 3 to 5 is the consensus, and more adds nothing proven.
- #Shorts uses one of the 3 slots shown above the title for no proven benefit. Replacing it with a topic hashtag such as #historia is a reasonable test. If kept, put it last.
- Hashtags in the title take visible characters from a roughly 40-character mobile title. Keep them in the description.
- Trending or event hashtags (#Halloween, #DiaDeMuertos, #IA) are where Sherman says hashtags can matter, so use them on topical episodes.

### Gaps
- There is no current (2025-2026) official YouTube statement specifically on whether #Shorts still helps.
- There is no controlled public study on hashtag count versus Shorts reach. The numeric thresholds ("drops above 8") are vendor claims without data.

## 2. Which Spanish hashtags are high-volume by theme?

### Takeaway
No verifiable volume data was obtainable. YouTube hashtag pages (youtube.com/hashtag/...) render with JavaScript and returned no counts when fetched. The Spanish "popular hashtag" lists found are generic vendor lists with no figures. The bank in section 6 is built from topic relevance and common Spanish usage, not measured volume. The owner should check counts manually.

### Cited Findings
- Fetching https://www.youtube.com/hashtag/historia returned only footer content and no video or channel counts. https://m.youtube.com/hashtag/curiosidades redirects to the desktop page. — direct fetch attempt (see URLs)
- [CREATOR/VENDOR] Cyberclick lists #youtube, #youtuber, #youtubeshorts, #viral and #trending among the most-used tags. Filmora's 2026 Shorts list proposes #Shorts, #viral, #trending and #fyp. Neither gives volume figures. — [Cyberclick](https://www.cyberclick.es/numerical-blog/hashtags-de-redes-sociales-para-guia-de-los-mas-relevantes); [Filmora ES](https://filmora.wondershare.es/youtube/youtube-shorts-hashtags.html)
- [CREATOR/VENDOR] Schedulala's Spanish examples: for education, #ciencia #datosdelespacial #astronomia; for entertainment, #comedia #skitcomedia. It suggests the pattern of area (#ciencia/#historia), then specific (#fisica), then format (#explicado). — [Schedulala ES](https://schedulala.com/es/blog/hashtags-youtube-shorts-mejores-practicas)
- [CREATOR/VENDOR] Filmora says checking trending videos is one of the most effective ways to find current hashtags. — [Filmora ES](https://filmora.wondershare.es/youtube/youtube-shorts-hashtags.html)
- [CREATOR/VENDOR] Opus Pro notes AI content growing with #AITools, #ChatGPT and #ArtificialIntelligence, all English. — [Opus Pro ES](https://www.opus.pro/es-es/blog/maximize-your-views-choosing-the-right-hashtags-for-youtube-shorts)

### Inferences
- Generic tags (#viral, #trending, #fyp) have huge competition and fail YouTube's "directly related" rule in spirit. Avoid them.
- For a LatAm audience, Spanish topic tags (#historia, #curiosidades, #ciencia) are likely better than English equivalents. This is unverified by data.
- A manual check is possible. Open youtube.com/hashtag/<tag> in a logged-in browser, where the header shows counts of videos and channels, or use the vidIQ or TubeBuddy extension. Record the counts before finalising the bank.

### Gaps
- There are no verified view or video counts for any Spanish hashtag. I did not invent numbers.
- No vidIQ or TubeBuddy Spanish-hashtag data was accessible without an account.

## 3. Description length and structure for Shorts, and whether repeated boilerplate hurts

### Takeaway
On Shorts, viewers barely see the description, but YouTube reads it as a topical and search signal. Keep it short: 1 to 2 natural keyword sentences, then sources, then 3 to 5 hashtags at the end. YouTube does not officially penalise a single repeated channel line. However, its policy prohibits "repetitive sentences in the description," and the line adds no topical signal. Cutting it or making it unique per episode is safer and cleaner.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] The title, thumbnail and description are "more important pieces of metadata for your video's discovery" than tags. — [YouTube Help: tags](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402?hl=en)
- [OFFICIAL] "Repetitive sentences in the description" are prohibited, under the hashtag and metadata policy. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en)
- [OFFICIAL] The spam policy targets "high-volume, repetitive, or deceptive" comments and mass-produced near-identical content. A brief channel tagline is not cited as an example. — [YouTube spam policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973?hl=en)
- [CREATOR/VENDOR] The collapsed description matters little to viewers, but YouTube's crawler reads it as a topical signal for search classification. Vendors advise against pasting lists of keyword variants. — summarised from [Subscribr SEO guide](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-shorts-seo-guide) and [Metadata Reactor 2026](https://metadatareactor.com/blog/youtube-shorts-seo-guide/), via search results
- [CREATOR/VENDOR] Vendors say Shorts have appeared in regular YouTube search, Google search and video carousels since 2023-2024, so description keywords help search traffic. — search summary of [ClipSpeed 2026](https://www.clipspeed.ai/blog/youtube-shorts-seo-optimization-guide.html)

### Inferences
- The repeated 30-word tagline most likely does not trigger a penalty: it is one sentence, not keyword stuffing. Still, it is wasted space and makes channel-wide copy obvious. Move it to the channel About section, or shorten it to a brand line of about 6 words, such as "Todo es verdad. Las caras no.", if the owner wants to keep a signature.
- Sources are good for credibility and for YouTube's information-quality context. Place them after the topical sentence so the first line stays about the topic.
- Make the first line a full sentence containing the main search terms (person, event, year), not a repeat of the title.

### Gaps
- There is no official YouTube statement on an ideal Shorts description length.

## 4. Title practices for Shorts

### Takeaway
Titles matter for search and for the first impression, less for the swipe feed. Aim for about 20 to 50 characters, with the main keyword (person or event) in the first 40 characters. Use a curiosity gap that stays truthful, since misleading titles violate the clickbait policy. Put no hashtags in the title. Emoji have no evidence for or against them.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] The title maximum is 100 characters. — [YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/10059070?hl=en)
- [OFFICIAL] Titles must not promise what the video doesn't deliver (malicious clickbait). — [YouTube spam policy](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973?hl=en)
- [CREATOR/VENDOR, updated June 2026] vidIQ's practical target is 20 to 40 characters. Mobile truncates around 40 characters and desktop around 55 to 70. It says to put the primary keyword in the first 40 characters and to use a question or intriguing adjective for curiosity. Its analysis of more than 10,000 trending Shorts found an average title length of about 51 characters, with the best-performing band at 4 to 6 words or 20 to 40 characters, excluding hashtags. Emoji are not addressed. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-shorts-titles/)
- [CREATOR/VENDOR] In-feed distribution is driven mostly by swipe and retention behaviour, not the title. A title can help viewers find a Short but "cannot guarantee a search position or Shorts feed distribution." — search summary of [Subscribr titles](https://subscribr.ai/youtube-strategy/youtube-shorts-titles-thumbnails-matter) and [ShortsFast](https://shortsfast.com/blog/write-youtube-shorts-titles-for-search/)

### Inferences
- Spanish title pattern: [Keyword/subject] + [absurd hook], for example "Australia perdió una guerra contra emús" or "¿Napoleón vs. 3000 conejos?". Use proper Spanish accents and opening question or exclamation marks (¿ ¡), because people search with and without accents. Tags can cover the unaccented spelling, which is the "misspelling" case.
- At most one emoji, at the end, purely stylistic. There is no data either way.

### Gaps
- There is no data on emoji effects or on Spanish-specific title length, since vidIQ's data is language-agnostic.

## 5. Tags field relevance

### Takeaway
Officially, tags are minimal. They are useful mainly for common misspellings. Spend under 30 seconds on them: add accentless and misspelled variants of names.

### Cited Findings
- [OFFICIAL] Tags "play a minimal role in your video's discovery," and "can be useful if the content of your video is commonly misspelled." — [YouTube Help: tags](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/146402?hl=en)
- [CREATOR/VENDOR] vidIQ's 2019 case study concluded that tags did not affect search ranking. TubeCore (2026) calls them a minor but measurable factor. — [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-keyword-tags-2019-case-study); [TubeCore](https://tubecore.xpanddigital.io/blog/youtube-tags-guide)

### Inferences
- For this channel, useful tags are misspellings and accentless forms, such as "calígula / caligula", "diogenes", "tycho brahe / tico brahe" and "emus guerra".

### Gaps
- None material.

## 6. Recommended template and hashtag bank (synthesis; built from the findings above, no volume data)

### Takeaway
The recommended description has about 3 parts and about 300 to 500 characters, sources included. The hashtag bank is relevance-based. Validate the volume of each hashtag manually on youtube.com/hashtag/<tag> before relying on it.

### Cited Findings
- Structure rules come from: hashtags at the end of the description and the 3 shown above the title ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en); [Schedulala](https://schedulala.com/es/blog/hashtags-youtube-shorts-mejores-practicas)); broad, then niche, then specific ([Schedulala](https://schedulala.com/es/blog/hashtags-youtube-shorts-mejores-practicas)); no repetitive sentences ([YouTube Help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/6390658?hl=en)).

### Inferences
Recommended description template (Spanish output):
```
{1-2 frases únicas con palabras clave: quién, qué, cuándo, dónde. Ej.: "En 1932, Australia le declaró la guerra a los emús… y perdió."}

Fuentes:
- {url 1}
- {url 2}

#{amplio} #{nicho} #{específico}
```
- Drop the repeated channel paragraph. Optionally keep a 1-line signature ("Todo es verdad. Las caras no."). Put the hashtags last, because the first 3 are the ones shown above the title, so order them by preference. Leave #Shorts out, or put it last.

Hashtag bank (relevance-based, volume unverified). Pick 1 broad, 1 niche and 1 to 2 specific per video:
- **History (broad):** #historia #historiaenespañol #datoshistoricos #historiauniversal. Specific: #napoleon #romaantigua #edadmedia #segundaguerramundial, or the person's name (#caligula #diogenes)
- **Curiosities:** #curiosidades #datoscuriosos #sabiasque #datosrandom #historiasreales
- **Science/space:** #ciencia #astronomia #espacio #nasa #agujerosnegros #fisica
- **Tech/AI:** #inteligenciaartificial #IA #tecnologia #chatgpt #anthropic #cripto
- **Economy/current affairs:** #economia #finanzas #apuestas #noticias
- **Halloween / Día de Muertos:** #halloween #diademuertos #diadelosmuertos #todoslossantos #cancioncriolla (Peru, 31 Oct, the Día de la Canción Criolla, is relevant for Lima episodes)
- **Peru/LatAm:** #peru #lima #peruanos #latinoamerica #historiadelperu
- **Humor/animation:** #humor #comedia #animacion #sketch #humorhistorico
- **Avoid:** #viral #trending #fyp #parati (generic, not "directly related", and TikTok-native per Schedulala), and any hashtag unrelated to the episode.

### Gaps
- No hashtag in this bank has verified volume. Next step: the owner opens each in a logged-in browser or the vidIQ extension and records the video and channel counts.
